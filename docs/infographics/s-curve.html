THE S-CURVE OF PROGRESS
Progress compounds like interest — exponential growth until the pocket shrinks.

COMPOUNDING

Consistent effort plus small wins stacks like compounded interest. The better the basics get, the more progress each rep buys. Growth is invisible at first.

THE TAKEOFF

Basics click and progress goes exponential. Skills that felt impossible start working. This is the steep part of the curve.

THE SHRINKING POCKET

At a high level, progress can only be small things. Adding a half turn to a triple full is much harder than adding a half twist to a layout. Small wins are exactly what to aim for.

Teach athletes that small wins are the target. When they focus on those kinds of wins and appreciate them, the curve keeps climbing.
